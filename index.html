How it works

More than a news feed. Someone to think it through with.

She learns what matters to you

Tell Gia what you’re planning or working on, in your own words. She works out which news touches your life — and you can change her focus anytime.

She explains, not just repeats

Gia weighs reporting from many sources, then tells you what most likely happened, why it matters to you, and what is still uncertain.

You can simply talk

Ask a question in the middle of a briefing, by voice or text. Gia answers, then picks up right where she left off.

You stay in control.

Gia is built on trust, not on keeping you scrolling.

  • Sources are always one tap away.
  • She says clearly when something isn’t confirmed.
  • She remembers your preferences only with your permission.
  • See what she knows about you — and change or delete it anytime.
