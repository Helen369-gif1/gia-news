How it works

More than a news feed. Someone to think it through with.

01Understands you

She learns what matters to you

Tell Gia what you’re planning or working on, in your own words. She works out which news touches your life — and you can change her focus anytime.

02Connects the dots
✳

She explains, not just repeats

Gia weighs reporting from many sources, then tells you what most likely happened, why it matters to you, and what is still uncertain.

03Always a conversation

Ask about anything

Not only your topics. Heard about a story somewhere? Ask Gia, by voice or text, and she’ll explain it the same way.
